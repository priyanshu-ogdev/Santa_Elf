## 2. Prize Structure ($50,000 USD Total)

| Prize Category | Amount | Details |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1st Place** | $12,000 | Awarded to the top-ranking team on the final leaderboard. |
| **2nd Place** | $10,000 | Awarded to the 2nd place team on the final leaderboard. |
| **3rd Place** | $10,000 | Awarded to the 3rd place team on the final leaderboard. |
| **4th Place** | $8,000 | Awarded to the 4th place team on the final leaderboard. |
| **Rudolph Prize** | $10,000 | Special achievement award (details below). |

### The Rudolph Prize Rules
* The **Rudolph Prize ($10,000)** is awarded to the team that maintains the **1st place position on the public leaderboard for the longest cumulative duration** throughout the competition (from launch until the final submission deadline).
* This incentivizes early sharing, aggressive continuous improvement, and active competition participation throughout the two-month duration.

---

## 3. Submission Limits & Rules

* **Daily Submissions:** Standard Kaggle limit (typically up to 5 submissions per team per day).
* **Final Submissions Selection:** Teams may select up to **2 final submissions** for official evaluation.
* **External Data & Tools:** Open-source solvers (e.g., OR-Tools CP-SAT, Z3, CBC), custom search algorithms, and machine learning models are permitted, provided they adhere to Kaggle's open-source licensing rules.
